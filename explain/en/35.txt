35.1.0|Habakkuk asks the Lord how long he must cry out about violence while the Lord does not seem to save. The Lord answers that He is raising up the Chaldeans, a fierce and fast nation, who will sweep across the land. Habakkuk is troubled, because the Lord is holy yet seems silent while the wicked swallow up those more righteous. He wonders if they will keep on taking nations forever.|Doctrine and Covenants 121:1-3 records a prayer asking God where He is and how long His people must suffer wrongs, which shows that faithful people may ask hard questions of God.|35.2.1-4,19.13.1-2,19.73.1-17,46.13.12|Have you ever asked God how long?~How can you keep your trust in Him when answers take time?
35.2.0|Habakkuk waits on his watchtower for the Lord's answer. The Lord tells him to write the vision plainly and to wait for it, and says the just shall live by his faith. Then come five woes against a proud conqueror who takes what is not his and builds with blood. The Lord says the earth will be filled with the knowledge of His glory as waters cover the sea.|2 Nephi 30:15 uses the same words about the earth being full of the knowledge of the Lord as the waters cover the sea.|45.1.17,58.10.38,23.11.9,23.44.9-20|What does it mean to live by faith?~How do you stay patient while you wait on the Lord?
35.3.0|Habakkuk prays and asks the Lord to renew His work and remember mercy. He sees the Lord's glory come like light, with the earth shaking and nations driven apart, and he remembers how the Lord saved His people. Habakkuk shakes with fear. Then he says that even if the fig tree does not bloom and the fields give no food, he will still rejoice in the Lord.||19.18.32-33,2.15.1-18,50.4.4,19.46.1-3|What helps you rejoice when things are hard?~How has the Lord been your strength?
