51.1.0|Paul thanks God for the faith and love of the saints at Colosse and prays that they will grow in knowledge and be fruitful. He describes Jesus Christ as the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation, by whom all things were made. Christ is the head of the church, the first to rise from the dead. Through Him we are reconciled to God and forgiven. Paul says he is glad to serve and to suffer for the church.|Moses 1:33 teaches that worlds were created by the Son, the Only Begotten. 2 Nephi 2:8 teaches that He is the first that should rise from the dead.|43.1.3,58.1.2-3,45.8.29|What does it mean to you that Jesus Christ created all things?~Where do you need to feel forgiven and made new?
51.2.0|Paul tells the saints how hard he works for them and wants their hearts to be comforted and united. He warns them not to be led away by clever words or the traditions of men. They are to keep walking in Christ, who is the head over all. He says they were buried with Christ in baptism and raised with Him. Paul tells them not to let others judge them over food, holy days, or strict rules that come from men.|Doctrine and Covenants 128:12 teaches that baptism by immersion is in the likeness of the resurrection of the dead.|45.6.3-4,43.1.18,40.15.9|How does baptism remind you of the Savior?~How can you tell the difference between what comes from Christ and what only comes from men?
51.3.0|Paul tells the saints to set their hearts on things above. They are to put away anger, lying, and bad speech, like old clothes. They are to put on kindness, humility, patience, and forgiveness, and above all, charity. He tells them to let the peace of God rule in their hearts and to do all things in the name of the Lord. He gives short counsel to wives, husbands, children, fathers, and servants.|Moroni 7:47 teaches that charity is the pure love of Christ.|49.4.32,40.6.14-15,40.22.37-39|Which old habit would you like to put away?~How can you show charity to someone in your family?
51.4.0|Paul asks the saints to keep praying and to be thankful. He asks them to pray that he can speak well about Christ even in prison. He tells them to be wise with outsiders and kind in how they speak. He sends greetings from friends, including Tychicus, Onesimus, Aristarchus, Mark, Justus, Epaphras, Luke, and Demas. He asks that this letter be read also in Laodicea.||55.4.11,57.1.10-12,44.12.12|How can your words be kind and also honest?~Who has helped you in the gospel, and have you thanked them?
