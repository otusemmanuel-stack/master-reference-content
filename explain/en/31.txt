31.1.0|Obadiah gives a message to Edom, the nation of Esau's family. Edom was proud and thought no one could bring it down, but the Lord says He will. Edom stood by when strangers took Jerusalem, rejoiced over Judah's trouble and blocked those who fled. The Lord says Edom will be repaid, and the kingdom will be the Lord's.||1.25.23-26,19.137.7,24.49.7-22,20.16.18|Why is it wrong to rejoice at someone else's trouble?~How can pride blind us?
