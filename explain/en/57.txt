57.1.0|Paul writes a short and warm letter to Philemon. Onesimus, a servant who had left Philemon, has become like a son to Paul while Paul is in chains. Paul sends him back and asks Philemon to receive him no longer as a servant but as a beloved brother. He offers to pay anything Onesimus owes. Paul trusts that Philemon will do even more than he asks.||51.4.9,49.6.5-9,40.18.21-22|When have you needed someone to forgive you?~How can you treat others as brothers and sisters in the gospel?
