37.1.0|Haggai speaks to the governor Zerubbabel and the high priest Joshua in the second year of King Darius. The people say it is not time to rebuild the Lord's house, but they live in fine houses. The Lord says, Consider your ways, and tells them to bring wood and build His house. Zerubbabel, Joshua and the people obey, and the Lord says, I am with you.|Doctrine and Covenants 95:8 records that the Lord gave a commandment in this dispensation to build a house in which He would endow those He has chosen with power from on high.|15.3.8-13,15.5.1-2,20.3.9-10,40.6.33|What do you put first in your life?~How can you help in the work of the Lord's house?
37.2.0|A month later Haggai speaks to those who remember the first temple and feel the new one looks like nothing. The Lord tells them to be strong and work, because He is with them, and He will fill this house with glory. Haggai uses the law about holy and unclean things to show that their work was spoiled by sin. From the day the foundation was laid, the Lord says He will bless them, and He promises to make Zerubbabel like a signet ring.||15.3.10-13,11.8.27-30,58.12.26-27,38.4.6-9|Why does the Lord ask us to keep working when things look small?~What blessing do you see from serving in His house?
