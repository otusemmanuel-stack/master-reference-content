24.51.0|The Lord tells of Babylon's fall. He will send armies against her, and her gods and idols will be shown to be worth nothing. Babylon was like a golden cup in His hand that made the nations drunk, but now she is broken. The Lord tells His people to flee from the midst of her and to remember Him, and says He will take up their cause. 24.52.0|The book ends by telling how Jerusalem fell. Zedekiah rebelled against Babylon, and the army besieged the city for about two years until there was famine. The walls were broken, and Zedekiah was caught, saw his sons killed, and was blinded and taken to Babylon in chains. The temple and city were burned, its treasures were carried off, and many people were taken captive.
