36.1.0|Zephaniah speaks in the days of King Josiah. The Lord says He will sweep away the worship of Baal and the stars, and those who have turned from Him. People who think the Lord will do nothing will lose their homes and goods. The day of the Lord is near, a day of distress and darkness, and silver and gold will not save them.||12.22.1-20,12.23.4-5,19.115.4-8,24.7.4|Why can money not save us in the end?~How do you seek the Lord each day?
36.2.0|Zephaniah calls the nation to seek the Lord before His anger comes. He tells the meek of the earth to seek righteousness and meekness, and perhaps they will be hidden in the day of the Lord's anger. Philistine cities, Moab, Ammon, Ethiopia and Assyria will be brought low for pride. Nineveh, which said that there was none beside her, will become a place for animals.||34.1.7,32.3.5-10,23.13.19-22,40.5.5|What does it mean to seek meekness?~Why does pride so often come before a fall?
36.3.0|Zephaniah first speaks against Jerusalem, a city that did not listen, would not take correction and did not trust in the Lord. Its leaders, prophets and priests are corrupt, but the Lord is just and does no wrong. He promises to purify the people's speech and to leave a humble and poor people who trust in the Lord. The chapter ends with joy, because the Lord is mighty, will save, and will rejoice over them with singing.||36.1.12,24.31.31-34,23.12.1-6,42.15.4-7|What does it mean that the Lord rejoices over you?~How can you be a person who trusts in the Lord?
