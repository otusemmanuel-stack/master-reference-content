47.2.0|Paul explains that he did not come to Corinth again so as not to cause more sorrow. He wrote with many tears, not to hurt them but to show his love. 47.3.0|Paul says the Corinthian saints are his letter of recommendation, written on hearts and not on stone, by the Spirit of the living God. The law written on stone had glory, so the ministry of the Spirit has more. God, who made light shine out of darkness, has shone in their hearts to give the light of the knowledge of His glory in the face of Jesus Christ. They are sorrowful yet always rejoicing, poor yet making many rich. Paul opens his heart to the Corinthians and asks them to open theirs.
