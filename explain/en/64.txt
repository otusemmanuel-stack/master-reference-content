64.1.0|John, called the elder, writes to his dear friend Gaius. He prays that Gaius will prosper and be in health, and he is glad that Gaius walks in the truth, for he has no greater joy than to hear that his children walk in truth. He praises Gaius for kindness to traveling brethren and strangers. He says a man named Diotrephes loves to be first, will not receive them, and speaks against them. John says to follow what is good, and he speaks well of Demetrius.||43.13.34-35,45.12.13,62.3.17-18|Who has been a Gaius to you, someone who welcomed and helped you?~How can you do good quietly, without needing praise?
