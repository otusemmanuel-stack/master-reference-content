59.2.0|James tells the saints not to show favor to the wealthy over the poor. He says that to love your neighbor as yourself is the royal law. He teaches that mercy matters, and that the one who shows no mercy will receive none. He then says faith without works is dead, giving the example of someone who sees a hungry brother and only says be warm and fed. He points to Abraham and Rahab as people whose faith was shown by what they did.|Mosiah 4:16 says we will help those who stand in need and will not turn the beggar away. 59.3.0|James warns that those who teach will be judged more strictly, and that we all make mistakes. He says the tongue is small but powerful, like a bit in a horse's mouth or a rudder on a ship. It can start a great fire. No one can tame it alone, and the same mouth should not both bless God and curse people. James contrasts earthly envy and strife with the wisdom from above, which is pure, peaceable, gentle, and full of mercy.|Mosiah 4:30 teaches us to watch our thoughts, our words, and our deeds, and Alma 12:14 says our words will condemn us if we do wrong.|20.15.1,40.12.36-37,49.4.29|What kind of words do you want to speak at home?~How can you be a peacemaker?
59.4.0|James asks where fights among them come from, and says it is from selfish desires, and that they ask for the wrong reasons. He tells them to submit to God, resist the devil, and draw near to God. He asks them to purify their hearts and be humble, and the Lord will lift them up. He tells them not to speak evil of each other or judge, and not to boast about tomorrow, since life is like a vapor. Whoever knows to do good and does not do it, sins.|Doctrine and Covenants 88:63 says to draw near to the Lord and He will draw near to us, to seek Him diligently and we shall find Him.|40.4.10-11,60.5.5-6,45.14.10-12|What helps you draw close to God?~What good thing do you know to do that you have been putting off?
59.5.0|James warns wealthy men who cheated their workers that their wealth will not last. He tells the saints to be patient until the Lord comes, like a farmer waiting for the harvest, and points to the prophets and to Job as examples of patience. He tells them to keep their word, to pray when troubled, and to sing when happy. If any are sick, they should call for the elders of the church, who will pray and anoint with oil. He ends by saying that anyone who brings back a sinner saves a soul.|Doctrine and Covenants 42:44 says the elders of the church, two or more, shall pray for the sick and lay their hands upon them in the Lord's name.|41.6.13,11.17.1,11.18.42-45|When has prayer helped you in a time of need?~How can you help someone come back to the Lord?
