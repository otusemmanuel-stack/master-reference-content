53.1.0|Paul, Silvanus, and Timothy write again to the saints at Thessalonica. They thank God that the saints' faith and love are growing, even though they are being persecuted. They say God will give rest to those who are troubled and will judge those who do not obey the gospel when the Lord is revealed from heaven. They pray that the saints will be found worthy of their calling.||43.16.33,40.5.10-12,58.12.1|How can trouble help your faith grow?~What gives you peace when others treat you unfairly?
53.2.0|Some people were saying that the day of Christ had already come. Paul tells the saints not to be shaken or fooled. He says that first there must be a falling away and a man of sin who opposes God must be revealed. Paul says God has chosen the saints for salvation, and he tells them to hold fast to what they were taught.|Doctrine and Covenants 1:15 describes a time when people have strayed from the Lord's ordinances and broken His everlasting covenant.|40.24.4-5,44.20.29-30,54.4.1|How can you tell what is true when many voices are teaching?~What teachings will you hold fast to?
53.3.0|Paul asks the saints to pray for him and for the word of the Lord to spread. He trusts the Lord to keep them from evil. He reminds them that he and his friends worked hard so they would not be a burden. He says that if anyone will not work, he should not eat, and he asks the idle to work quietly. He tells the saints not to grow weary in doing good and to treat a person who will not listen as a brother.|Doctrine and Covenants 42:42 says that the idle shall not eat the bread or wear the garments of the laborer. Doctrine and Covenants 64:33 says to be not weary in well-doing.|52.4.11-12,49.4.28,48.6.9|Why is honest work a blessing?~How can you correct someone with love?
