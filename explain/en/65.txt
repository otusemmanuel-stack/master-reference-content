65.1.0|Jude, the brother of James, urges the believers to contend for the faith once given to the saints. Some ungodly men have crept in and turn God's grace into an excuse to sin. Jude reminds them of examples from the past, including Israel in the wilderness, angels who left their place, and Sodom and Gomorrah. He describes the false teachers as grumblers who follow their own desires. He tells the saints to build themselves up in faith, pray in the Holy Ghost, keep in the love of God, and help those who doubt.|Moses 7:65 says that Enoch saw the day of the coming of the Son of Man in the last days.|1.5.21-24,61.2.4-6,66.12.7-9|How can you build yourself up in the faith?~How can you show mercy to someone who has doubts?
