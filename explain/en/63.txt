63.1.0|John writes to a chosen lady and her children, and he is glad that some of her children are walking in truth. He tells her again to love one another and to walk in the commandments. He warns that deceivers have gone out into the world who deny that Jesus Christ came in the flesh. He tells them to abide in the doctrine of Christ and not to welcome teachers who do not bring it. John hopes to visit soon.|2 Nephi 31:21 says this is the doctrine of Christ, and the only and true doctrine of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Ghost.|43.13.34-35,43.14.15,55.3.14|What is the doctrine of Christ in your own words?~How can you walk in truth in your home?
